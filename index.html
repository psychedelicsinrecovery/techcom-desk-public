Instead of getting lost in a chat thread, your request becomes a private thread with the Tech Committee and a tracked ticket like TECH-12.

What this desk handles

Bring us these

  • Problems with the websites (psychedelicsinrecovery.org and the service site)
  • Email, forms, Zoom and calendar issues
  • Privacy and data requests (with the Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship subcommittee)

Something else? Ask A.L.E.X. first. Under any answer, Turn into a ticket suggests the right desk.

Open a ticket

Three steps

  1. Join PIR®'s Discord.
  2. Type and choose Problem or request or Idea or feedback.
  3. A private thread opens between you and the committee. Check on it any time with /ticket id:TECH-12.
Every ticket's lifecycle

new→ claimed→ in progress→ resolved→ archived

Only the Tech Committee can change a ticket's status. Resolved threads move to the channel's Threads → Archived list, and Reopen brings them back.

Not on Discord?

Use the web desk: enter your email, tap the one-time link we send you, and open or follow a ticket. No password, no account. Or email helpdesk@psychedelicsinrecovery.org.

United States

  • Emergency: call 911
  • Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988 (veterans: 988 then press 1)
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741
  • Fireside Project (support during/after a challenging psychedelic experience): call or text 62-FIRESIDE (623-473-7433), 11am–11pm Pacific

United Kingdom

  • Samaritans: 116 123 (free, 24/7)

More lines (other countries, domestic violence, SAMHSA): https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/crisis-resources/

I'm an automated helper, not a crisis service, and PIR is not a hotline. Please reach out to one of the lines above or to someone you trust right now.
